## Brand & Style
The design system operates at the nexus of speculative military aerospace avionics, industrial cyber-physical infrastructure, and tactical SCADA control interfaces. It rejects conventional corporate design tropes in favor of an uncompromising, mission-critical cyberpunk console aesthetic engineered for high cognitive throughput, zero-latency situational comprehension, and round-the-clock data surveillance.

The aesthetic profile unites **High-Density Sci-Fi Telemetry** with **Tactical Dark Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-deep obsidian and void foundations nullify optic fatigue in multi-monitor, low-illumination operational command facilities.
- Phosphor neon accents—electric cyan, radioactive hyper-green, solar amber, and laser crimson—operate as immediate informational beacons and target acquisitions.
- Structural geometry features razor-thin 1px glow perimeters, chamfered corner cuts, visual targeting reticles, HUD bracket markers (`[ + ]`, `┌ ┐`), and coordinate readouts.
- Multilayered frosted dark glass panels and subtle cyan-tinted scanline textures evoke an authenticated cockpit holographic projection.

## Colors
The color architecture is built around absolute low-luminance obsidian foundations punctuated by high-potency spectral emitters. Every chromatic hue serves an explicit semantic diagnostic role:

- **Primary Cyan (`#00f0ff`)**: The central interactive axis—targeting locks, telemetry vectors, primary data channels, and operational links.
- **Secondary Hyper-Green (`#00ff66`)**: Nominal system states, verified SCADA protocols, stable sensor throughput, and active heartbeat loops.
- **Tertiary Solar Amber (`#ffb700`)**: Diagnostic warnings, threshold deviations, air-gap advisory locks, and transient network latency.
- **Neutral Void (`#03070c`)**: Deep space optical baseline, stepped into structural layers `#070d14` and `#0b131e`.

### Auxiliary Alert Channels
- **Critical Laser Crimson (`#ff2a5f`)**: Hard alerts, active payload breaches, catastrophic failover, and lethal override commands.
- **Plasma Purple (`#a855f7`)**: Auxiliary telemetry pipelines, encrypted sensor streams, quantum channel locks, and specialized peripheral telemetry.

Color application must follow strict hairline discipline: neon tones are delivered via 1px outer borders, glowing indicators, micro-metrics, and subtle transparent backdrops. Solid neon fills are strictly limited to active, high-priority state changes.

## Typography
The system enforces a strict dual-engine typographic protocol:

1. **Space Grotesk (Command & Briefing Context)**: Used for layout headers, module titles, system logs, and executive operational summaries. Its geometric quirks and sharp angled cuts provide a future-forward industrial aesthetic while sustaining immediate legibility.
2. **JetBrains Mono (Telemetry, Hex Coordinates, & Micro-Labels)**: Applied to SCADA values, dynamic gauges, status pips, timecodes, vector coordinates, and input fields. Zero-character ambiguity (differentiating zero from capital O, and 1 from l) eliminates operational risk during active intervention.

Labels must render in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to replicate instrument-panel engraving and avionic cockpit screens.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture is configured for maximum information density and spatial efficiency across large multi-display matrix arrays and mobile field decks:

- **Desktop & Command Matrix (1440px+)**: 12-column or 16-column modular fluid grid with an outer canvas margin of `1.5rem` (`margin-desktop`) and gutters locked to `1rem` (`gutter-desktop`). Visual density allows operator oversight of dozens of parallel telemetry vectors without vertical scrolling.
- **Tablet / Secondary Rig (768px – 1439px)**: 8-column layout with `0.75rem` gutters. Peripheral telemetry docks collapse into side drawers or horizontal ribbon arrays.
- **Tactical Field Terminal (< 768px)**: Single-column linear layout maintaining an edge margin of `1rem`. Critical alert tickers and targeting scopes lock to sticky top/bottom anchors.

Internal component spacing adheres to compressed rhythms (`space-xs` to `space-md`), minimizing empty visual space in favor of compact, structured HUD arrays.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is achieved through frosted dark glass surfaces, luminescent neon outlines, and chromatic aura layers—never standard blurred shadows.

- **Level 0 (Command Void)**: `#03070c` base background with faint CSS scanline stripes (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.015)` alternating 2px lines) and an overlay of subtle cyan/emerald coordinate reticle grids (32px grid pitch).
- **Level 1 (Telemetry Panels)**: Semi-transparent glass (`#070d14` at 80% opacity) with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`. Structural 1px border rendered in `#162536`.
- **Level 2 (Active Target / Focused Nodes)**: `#0b131e` at 90% opacity with razor 1px borders in `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)` and subtle radial cyan halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 15px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12), inset 0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Critical Breaches)**: Floating overlays encased in 1px laser-crimson borders (`#ff2a5f`) accompanied by emergency pulsating backglows (`0 0 25px rgba(255, 42, 95, 0.3)`).

## Shapes
The design system adopts a strict **Sharp (`0`)** corner philosophy. Soft, friendly, or organic rounded surfaces are eliminated entirely to uphold an uncompromising cyber-physical weapon system and avionic console vibe.

- **Chamfered Cuts**: Critical panels, status badges, buttons, and display containers utilize 45-degree angled chamfered corners (implemented via CSS `clip-path: polygon(...)`) ranging from 4px to 10px depending on module size.
- **HUD Brackets & Reticles**: Corners of high-density cards must be bookended with technical micro-brackets (`┌ ┐`, `└ ┘`) or crosshair ticks (`+`) positioned absolute to reinforce instrument optics.
- **Pills and circles** are banned with the sole exception of radar sweeps and micro-LED status dots (4px to 6px circular beacons).

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Actuators
- **Primary Cyber-Physical Button**: Razor 1px border in `#00f0ff`, dark obsidian background (`#070d14`), uppercase `JetBrains Mono` text in `#00f0ff`. Outer corner chamfers (6px). Hover triggers solid `#00f0ff` glow with `#03070c` text inversion and an active box-shadow flare (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Action**: `#0b131e` paneling, 1px border `#162536`, text in `#e2e8f0`. Hover converts border to `#00ff66` with green micro-sheen.
- **Destructive Breach Override**: Crimson styling (`#ff2a5f`), faint red glass background (`rgba(255, 42, 95, 0.1)`), 1px border `#ff2a5f`, with a rapid diagnostic border pulse animation on arming.

### Status Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Micro height (20px–22px), monospaced `label-sm` typography, enclosed inside hairline borders.
- Styled as `[ SYS // NOMINAL ]` or `[ ERR // 0x4F ]` paired with a glowing 4px LED pip.
- Emerald nominal state: `rgba(0, 255, 102, 0.08)` fill, `#00ff66` border (30% opacity).
- Crimson threat state: `rgba(255, 42, 95, 0.12)` fill, `#ff2a5f` border (60% opacity).

### Input Fields & Terminal Consoles
- Sharp 0px corners with chamfered bottom-right notch.
- Background in `#03070c`, border in `#162536`, text formatted with `JetBrains Mono`.
- Active focus invokes a 1px `#00f0ff` border, terminal block blinking cursor, and an understated cyan glow backdrop.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Selectors
- Checkboxes are 14px sharp squares framed by a 1px `#162536` border. Checked states feature a glowing `#00f0ff` central square core with an internal glow.
- Radio buttons use 45-degree rotated diamond geometry. Active selections display a solid `#00ff66` diamond node.

### HUD Data Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Framed with frosted dark glass (`#070d14` at 85% opacity, blur 12px) featuring HUD corner crosshairs (`[ + ]` accents) and top-rail hex coordinate markers (`SEC-01 // 77.294.02`).
- Integrated dynamic telemetry widgets pairing oversized monospaced numbers (`telemetry-metric`) with real-time vector graphs, step charts, and glowing cyan/emerald waveforms.
